# ADMIN_MANUAL.md — Administrator Operating Manual

## 1. Tenant Management & Super Admin Portal
* Log in as Platform Administrator (`admin@payrollpro.com`).
* Access **Super Admin Portal** to manage tenants:
  * Create new client tenants.
  * Enable/disable feature flags per tenant (`geolocation`, `recruitment`, `ess`, `ai`).
  * Modify employee allocation limits and subscription tiers.
  * Click **Impersonate Tenant** to troubleshoot client issues securely.

## 2. Policy Management
* Navigate to **Settings -> Policy Hub**.
* Configure company-wide policies across 16 domains:
  * Attendance grace periods & late penalty thresholds.
  * Overtime multipliers.
  * Leave encashment rules.
  * Notice period & probation defaults.

## 3. Running Monthly Payroll
* Navigate to **Payroll -> 5-Step Payroll Wizard**.
* Step 1: Select Pay Period & Branch.
* Step 2: Review Attendance & LOP Deductions.
* Step 3: Compute Statutory Deductions (EPF, ESIC, PT, TDS).
* Step 4: Preview Salary Slips & Bank Transfer File.
* Step 5: Lock & Authorize Batch Disbursement.

## 4. Statutory Filing & Compliance
* Navigate to **Compliance Hub**.
* Download EPFO ECR text files, ESIC return CSVs, and state Professional Tax statements for submission to government portals.
