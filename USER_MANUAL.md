# USER_MANUAL.md — End-User Operating Manual

## 1. Logging In
* Navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
* Enter your registered organization email, password, and tenant code.

## 2. Attendance & Geofenced Punching
* Open **Attendance Grid** from the sidebar navigation.
* Click **Punch In** or **Punch Out**. If enabled by your organization, allow browser location access.
* Out-of-fence punches will be submitted automatically with a status of "Flagged - Pending HR Review".

## 3. Leave Requests
* Open **Leave Management**.
* Click **Apply for Leave**. Select leave type (Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave), start date, end date, and reason.
* Monitor approval status in real-time.

## 4. Payslips & Tax Documents
* Open **Payroll -> Salary Slips**.
* Select the desired month to view and download itemized payslips showing Gross Pay, EPF, ESIC, Professional Tax, TDS, and Net Salary.
